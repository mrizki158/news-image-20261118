One image that sums up the world news of **Wednesday 18 November 2026** in **50 sourced stories**: four illustrated leads (the US–Iran Gulf escalation over Qeshm Island, the Trump–Xi–Putin Shenzhen summit, Ukraine's third Crimea Bridge strike in a week, Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO), ten illustrated tiles (the Democrats' 56–44 US Senate win, the US losing its measles-free status, Congo's Ebola outbreak passing 11,000 cases, Voyager 1 at one light-day, Bangladesh unrest, the Ethiopia–Eritrea border build-up, Imamoglu's political ban, the Quad in Manila, the 141-drone night over Ukraine, the Gaza ceasefire warning), and a 36-item world index beside a pinned dot-matrix world map — plus a markets strip and a UTC timeline of the day. The image is drawn programmatically (`build_image.py`, Pillow) from stories cross-checked against at least two independent reports dated that day; `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
